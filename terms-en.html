2. Description and scope of the Service

TabeWise is a Japan-first lifestyle information service. Its first function is to help you understand product information, starting with food: you scan a Japanese ingredient label or a barcode, and the App shows what it could read, explains the ingredients it recognises, and shows screening results with reasons.

The App currently provides: label scanning, barcode lookup, ingredient search, screening results with reasons, allergen display, a local scan history and favourites, English and Japanese languages, and several appearance themes. Features that are described as planned or in preparation on the website are not guaranteed and may change.

The Service is provided for general information only.

3. Product scanning and preliminary information

Results are produced from text that the App reads from a photo or a barcode lookup, and from a built-in ingredient dictionary. All results are preliminary. They depend on what the label states and on what the App can read, and they may be incomplete or wrong.

  • Text recognition can misread Japanese, especially on curved, glossy, dark or small labels.
  • Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts, a community database whose data can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect.
  • Labels often do not state where an ingredient comes from. When information is missing, the App shows it as unavailable and does not guess.
  • A product name is never treated as evidence of what a product contains.

4. Accuracy and limitations of product information

We work to improve the information in the App, but we do not promise that it is accurate, complete, current or suitable for any particular purpose. Product formulations change, and the information on the package is always the authority.

Always read the package itself. Do not rely on the Service alone for decisions that affect your health, safety or beliefs. If you have a food allergy, an intolerance or a medical condition, follow the advice of a qualified professional and read every label yourself.

5. Dietary, ingredient and religious screening limitations

The App screens ingredients against draft rules for dietary considerations such as halal, vegan and vegetarian.

  • The screening is a screening aid only. It is not halal certification, not a religious ruling (fatwa) and not an endorsement of any product.
  • The entries behind the screening are drafts. They have not been reviewed or approved by a qualified religious adviser.
  • The result "no concern identified" never means that a product is halal, vegan, vegetarian or allergen-free.
  • The "verified" levels are reserved for independently verified certification evidence, which the App cannot produce at this time.
  • TabeWise does not provide official certification of any kind and makes no claim of government or religious approval.

If a dietary or religious requirement is important to you, check the package, ask the manufacturer, or consult a qualified adviser.
